I am a postdoctoral associate at New York University Abu Dhabi, where I work with Taoufik Hmidi. Previously, I held a postdoctoral position at École Normale Supérieure de Lyon with Julien Vovelle.

I completed my PhD at Université Côte d’Azur under the supervision of Didier Clamond and Stéphane Junca. I subsequently held a teaching and research position (ATER) there.

Research interests

Hyperbolic PDEsStochastic PDEsWater wavesFractional BV spacesNonlinear equationsSingular limits

My research focuses on deterministic and stochastic nonlinear partial differential equations. I study the regularity of entropy solutions to hyperbolic equations, particularly in fractional BV spaces, as well as regularisations and singular limits. I also work on water-wave models in the shallow-water regime.

The watery theme continues in my name: Billel (Arabic: بلال) means “wet” or “dew”, while Guelmame is an Amazigh word meaning “lake” or “pond”.
